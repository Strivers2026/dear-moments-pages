Privacy Policy

Dear Moments – Photo Diary

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Dear Moments is a personal photo diary developed by Strivers Projects. We respect your privacy and aim to give you control over your personal memories.

1. Information You Provide

You may add photographs, diary entries, titles, and notes to the app. These are intended to be stored locally on your device.

2. Photos and Device Access

The app may request access to photos or use Android's photo picker when you choose an image. Selected photos are used to create your diary entries.

3. Google Drive Backup

Optional Google Drive backup is being developed. When available and enabled, it will use Google's authorization system and private application storage to back up your diary data and photos.

Google Drive backup will require your permission. You will be able to use the diary without connecting a Google account.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal diary entries or photos. We do not intentionally make your private memories publicly accessible.

5. Data Storage and Security

Local diary data is intended to remain on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup when the feature becomes available, backup data will be stored in your Google account's private app storage.

6. Data Deletion

You can request assistance with deleting your data by contacting us. Future versions are intended to provide controls for managing local data and Google Drive backups.

7. Children's Privacy

Dear Moments is designed as a general personal diary. It is not specifically directed toward children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
